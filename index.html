    Packing checklist

    Essential travel tips

    • Book transport early for high-season routes and local flights.
    • Keep a digital and physical copy of important travel documents.
    • Carry small cash for local markets and taxis in remote destinations.
    • Respect local customs, dress codes and daily time schedules.

    Local food recommendations

    • Street tacos with fresh salsa and lime
    • Fresh rice bowls and grilled seafood by the coast
    • Seasonal ramen and sushi tasting menus in Tokyo-style districts
    • Slow-cooked curries with regional spices and local breads

    Travel glossary

    Transit pass
    Useful for unlimited city movement on trains, buses and metro lines.
    Peak season
    High-demand travel months when prices and crowds rise significantly.
    Local guide
    A trained local expert who offers cultural, historical, and route insights.
